## What we collect
- **Account data:** your email address and the business profile you give us (sectors, keywords, region, contract-size preferences).
- **Billing data:** handled by **Stripe**. We never see or store your card details; we receive only your subscription status and the email tied to it.
- **Usage data:** basic, privacy-respecting analytics (pages viewed) to improve the service.

## Why we use it (lawful basis)
- To provide the service — matching live public contracts to your profile and emailing them (**contract**).
- To take payment and manage your subscription (**contract**).
- To improve the product and prevent abuse (**legitimate interests**).
We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## The contract data itself
The public contract notices we show come from Public Contracts Scotland and UK Contracts Finder under the Open Government Licence v3.0. That is public information, not personal data about you.

## Who we share with
Only the processors that run the service: **Stripe** (payments), **Resend** (sending your emails), **Supabase** (database hosting), and our site host. Each processes data on our instructions under its own data terms.

## Keeping and deleting
We keep your account data while your subscription is active and for a short period after, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing [privacy@bidbeacon.co.uk].

## Your rights
Under UK GDPR you can access, correct, delete, or port your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. Email us to exercise any of these. You can also complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at ico.org.uk.

## Email
Every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe. Unsubscribing stops the digests but keeps your account unless you ask us to close it.

## Changes
We'll post changes here and update the date above; significant changes will be emailed to members.
